## Summary

Wizard stepper was wrapping to a second line after the d9cde92 rebuild
added a 5th step (Brief). Tightened `.bb-stepper` so all 5 pills sit on
one row:

- `grid-template-columns` → 5 (was 4) so the grid actually accounts for
  the new step.
- Trimmed gap (12 → 8), padding (32 → 24), pill diameter (26 → 22),
  type sizes (strong 13 → 12, small 10 → 9), and column gap (9 → 8) so
  five pills fit on a 1280-px stage.
- Added `min-width: 0` plus `white-space: nowrap` + `text-overflow: ellipsis`
  on the strong/small children so labels stay on a single line and
  truncate gracefully on narrower viewports instead of wrapping.

## Blockers Or Risks

  ("Their face" → "Their f…"). Acceptable for now; we'd want a
  horizontal-scroll or hidden-on-narrow treatment if mobile becomes a
