## Summary

Fix "my recorded voice samples disappear" — two changes in
`components/creation-form.tsx`:

1. `startFresh` (the "Make a new video" button on the result screen) no
   longer wipes the voice draft or the cached ElevenLabs voice clone.
   Recording three guided takes is expensive UX; preserving them across
   "make another video" is the right default. The explicit "Re-record
   voice sample" button on the voice card is still the wipe path.

2. `voiceMode` is now part of the persisted voice draft. Without it,
   reload always landed on `narrate` and the speak-yourself recording
   UI was hidden — reading as "my samples are gone" even when the data
   was restored. Older entries without the field default to `narrate`
   on hydrate, so no migration is needed.

## Blockers Or Risks

None. The reset-flow test (`resets the flow when making a new video
from the result screen`) only asserts the user lands back on draft
phase with the photo dropzone — passes unchanged. All 48 tests green.

## Next Context

If the user still reports samples not detected after this lands, look
at the persist effect's quota error handling — `persistVoiceDraft`
silently swallows `QuotaExceededError` after one retry. Surfacing that
to the UI (a small banner: "your browser blocked storing voice samples
— check private-browsing or storage permissions") would be the next
defensive layer. Also worth investigating: clip-index mapping when
takes are recorded out of order (current `clipsData` filter drops
nulls, which corrupts index→clip mapping for partial-take cases).
